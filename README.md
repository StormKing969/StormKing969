### Hi, I'm Sajana 👋

I'm a software engineer who grew up moving around — born with roots in **Sri Lanka**, raised for a stretch on the island of **Mauritius**, and settled now on **Cape Cod, USA**. 🇱🇰 🇲🇺 🇺🇸 That mix left me pretty comfortable jumping into new environments, which turns out to be a decent trait for engineering too.

I spent three years at **General Motors** building backend systems for a platform used across 3,000+ dealerships — REST APIs, Kafka pipelines, the kind of work where a slow query or a bad migration affects a lot of people fast. These days I co-run **PolarisCove Studios**, a small web dev studio, where I take projects from a blank database through a deployed front end and everything in between, including the client sites nobody else wanted to touch because the previous developer left it in pieces.

I'm currently **open to full-time software engineering roles** — Java/Spring Boot backend, full-stack, or anything in between. Open to relocating, including internationally.
